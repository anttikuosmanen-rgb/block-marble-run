Studs and antistuds, as derived from each part's geometry.

  T  stud on top          o  antistud underneath
  B  both                 .  part of the piece, neither
  -  outside the piece's footprint   O  the hole a ball drops through

Rows run with +y upward, columns with +x rightward, as the part sits
unrotated. Grid layers are half a brick: a brick is 2, a plate 1.
